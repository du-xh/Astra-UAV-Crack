TransNeXt integration
=====================

The files transnext_native.py and transnext_cuda.py are derived from the
official TransNeXt repository by Dai Shi:
https://github.com/DaiShiResearch/TransNeXt
upstream commit c8a99743b60ac94ac8d2bf66ffe164a440dcfe21.

TransNeXt is licensed under Apache License 2.0.  The unmodified upstream
license and source snapshot are retained under third_party/TransNeXt.

Local modifications adapt imports and registry names to MMSegmentation 1.2.x,
allow the CUDA and native implementations to coexist, and remove an avoidable
CPU/GPU mismatch in relative-coordinate initialization and normalization.
